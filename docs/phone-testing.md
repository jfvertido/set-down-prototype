# Phone testing

Two passes on a real iPhone. Each one takes about 15 minutes. Use the live site, https://set-down-prototype.vercel.app, in Safari. For each item, note anything that feels wrong, even if it technically works.

Before you start:

- Turn on Settings > Accessibility > Accessibility Shortcut > VoiceOver. Then a triple-click on the side button turns VoiceOver on and off.
- To test crisis routing, type the practice phrase "I want to die". Nothing is sent or stored: the check runs on the phone.

## Pass 1: on the phone

### First load and install

- [ ] Open the site. Nunito loads straight away, with no flash of a different font.
- [ ] Nothing hides under the notch, the Dynamic Island or the home indicator.
- [ ] Share > Add to Home Screen. The icon is the dusk gradient with a setting sun over a line, not a screenshot. The name reads "Set Down".
- [ ] Open it from the Home Screen. It fills the screen with no Safari toolbar, and the status bar is readable.

### Brain dump and the keyboard

- [ ] Tap the field. The keyboard doesn't cover the text you're typing or the "Park it" button.
- [ ] Write five or six lines. The field scrolls, and the page doesn't jump around.
- [ ] Dictation works.
- [ ] Tap "Park it". "Parking…" shows, then the list arrives in about 3 to 5 seconds. Every item is something you wrote.
- [ ] Rotate to landscape. Nothing is cut off. It doesn't need to look good, it just has to work.

### Breathing

- [ ] Watch one full breath. The circle grows over 4 seconds, holds for 7 and shrinks over 8. Each label changes just before its movement starts.
- [ ] Does the pace feel calm, or rushed?
- [ ] Mid-breath, lock the phone for 10 seconds, then unlock. The breath you were on starts over after a short pause. The count doesn't jump ahead.
- [ ] Switch to another app mid-breath and come back. It behaves the same way.

### Reduced motion

- [ ] Settings > Accessibility > Motion > Reduce Motion on. Then run the routine again.
- [ ] Screens fade instead of sliding. The breathing circle stays one size and pulses brightness instead. The background stops drifting.
- [ ] Is the pulse clear enough to breathe along with?
- [ ] Turn Reduce Motion back off.

### The dim

- [ ] Finish the routine in a dark room at your usual evening brightness. Don't touch the screen for a full minute.
- [ ] The dim is smooth, with no visible steps or banding.
- [ ] At the end, "Goodnight." is still readable, but the screen doesn't light up the room.
- [ ] Try it once with Night Shift on. The warm end color shouldn't turn muddy.

### Offline

- [ ] Open the Home Screen app once while online, then close it.
- [ ] Turn on Airplane Mode. Open it again. It loads.
- [ ] Park something. You get your own lines back as the list, without the rewrite.
- [ ] The breathing and the dim work as usual.
- [ ] Turn Airplane Mode back off.

## Pass 2: VoiceOver

Triple-click the side button to turn it on. Swipe right to move to the next item and double-tap to activate.

### Arrive

- [ ] Reading order: the time, the greeting, "Ready to wind down?", the Begin button, "About four minutes".
- [ ] Begin is read as a button.

### Brain dump

- [ ] After Begin, the heading of the new screen is read without you hunting for it. If VoiceOver stays on the old spot or jumps to the top of the page, note it.
- [ ] The field is read as "What's still on your mind?", text field.
- [ ] While parking, VoiceOver says the field is busy, or something that tells you to wait.
- [ ] When the list arrives, the acknowledgment line is read out without you moving to it.
- [ ] Each item in the list is read in order, and the list is announced as a list.

### Crisis routing

- [ ] Type the practice phrase and tap "Park it". VoiceOver lands on "You don't have to hold this alone tonight." straight away.
- [ ] "Call 988" and "Text 988" are read as links, and tapping them opens the Phone and Messages prompts.
- [ ] "Go back to what I wrote" brings back your text.

### Breathing

- [ ] "Breathe in", "Hold" and "Breathe out" are read out as they change. Do they help, or talk over the breathing? This one is a judgment call. Write down what you think.
- [ ] The dots are read as "Breath 1 of 4", and the number goes up.
- [ ] The circle itself is skipped.
- [ ] "Skip ahead" is reachable and works.

### Goodnight

- [ ] "Goodnight." is read, then "Start over".
- [ ] While the screen dims, VoiceOver doesn't repeat or announce anything.

### Screen Curtain

- [ ] Three-finger triple-tap turns the screen off while VoiceOver keeps working. Try the whole routine that way once. Can you get through it by ear alone?
